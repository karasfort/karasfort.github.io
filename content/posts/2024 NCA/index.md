I am happy to share that Team Fluency received a Top Paper award from the Social Cognition division at NCA. Our paper investigated how code-mixing in a narrative can impede processing fluency and narrative engagement, resulting in poorer character attitudes and out-group evaluations. Thank you so much to my co-authors - Rachel, Elizabeth, Jorge, and Hillary - for their support during the presentation! 

You can read the full article [here](https://doi.org/10.1177/00936502241287334).
