Teaching

I believe that learning is an iterative process, in which success is only achieved through overcoming numerous challenges and failures. Because of this, I emphasize the importance of fallibility in my classes through the use of low-stakes, scaffolded assignments, activities that require students to learn about and use media literacy, and debate activities that require students to staunchly defend their positions. I also recognize that applying a theoretical lens to an applied and diverse field, such as communication, can be difficult, so I work to provide students opportunities to engage with applied scenarios to work through how theory can inform real-world processes.

Courses Taught

I have served as both an independent instructor and as a teaching assistant throughout my time at The Ohio State University. I have taught both online and in-person, across a variety of class sizes. I have been responsible for developing accelerated 8-week versions of courses and supervising graduate student graders.
